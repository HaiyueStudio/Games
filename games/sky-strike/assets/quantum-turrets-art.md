# Dreadnought articulated layers

Built-in image_gen was used to edit the existing battleship into an empty six-socket hull and to create a matching isolated three-barrel turret. The generated drafts contained baked checkerboards instead of alpha. For the requested app update, neutral bright background pixels connected to the image boundary were converted to transparent alpha. Retained RGB pixels are unchanged; source/result hashes and transparent-pixel counts are archived in `masters/quantum-turrets/alpha-preparation.json`.

The central decorative rotor is removed. The physical battleship now travels smoothly between both screen halves with a 12.57 s cycle; its echo moves in the opposite direction. Horizontal reach is 29% of the visible width (at most 140 logical pixels), adapting to narrow screens.

The runtime hull stays at 512 px, the shared turret at 128 px, and the original assembled carousel preview at 256 px. Six turret instances rotate independently; their barrel tips own projectile and muzzle-flash positions. The central reactor is drawn through engine primitives with a 2.2 s yellow breathing cycle, changing strictly below 35% health to a 0.42 s red cycle.

## Hull edit prompt

Edit target: attached top-down cosmic battleship sprite. Produce a clean BASE HULL animation layer, same square canvas, exact same silhouette, position, scale, nose pointing down, armor plating, gold trim, blue laser emitters and all other details. Remove ALL SIX complete rotating gun assemblies on the left and right edges: remove their long horizontal barrels AND their upper rotating armored gun heads. In each of the six original positions leave only a small flat recessed circular dark gunmetal mounting socket with a subtle brass bearing ring. Reconstruct the underlying hull armor, with NO barrels remaining anywhere on the six sockets. Three empty sockets on each side, exactly the original pivot locations. Keep central circular reactor frame and its four supporting spokes, but change ONLY the yellow glowing glass inside the center reactor to unlit DARK NEUTRAL GRAY glass with no orange/yellow glow; runtime will animate colored light. Preserve other amber trim details. TRUE TRANSPARENT alpha background. No labels, no diagram, no extra parts, no sprite sheet. This is an exact alignment layer for the original sprite, not a new ship design.

## Turret prompt

Reference image is a battleship; output ONLY ONE of its six side gun turrets, isolated as a reusable animation sprite on a TRUE TRANSPARENT background. Do not output the ship. Orthographic top-down game art. Match its dark titanium plated rotating armored gun head, brass/gold bearing rim, small cyan lights, and a battery of THREE long parallel barrels. Point all three barrels STRAIGHT UP toward the top of the square canvas. Composition is technically important: the center of the circular rotating gun head / rotation pivot must be at the exact center of the image (50% width, 50% height). The head is about 52% canvas width, the barrels reach from the head toward 8% canvas height, and the whole turret stays inside the frame. The rear of the head ends near 77% canvas height, leaving transparent bottom padding. No ground socket or hull deck below, no shadows outside the object, no exhaust, no muzzle flash, no labels, no text, no background, no sprite sheet. Crisp dimensional metallic art, recognizable at 40 pixels in game.
